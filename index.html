Legal Summarization

Plain English Summarization of Contracts - A dataset for automatic summarization of legal text

About the Project

This project provides a dataset for research in plain English summarization of legal contracts. It contains paired sets of original legal text and their corresponding human-written reference summaries, drawn from two community-driven legal simplification websites.

The dataset supports quantitative analysis of contract summarization as described in the associated paper presented at the NAACL Natural Legal Language Processing Workshop 2019.

Dataset Files

  • all_v1.json - 446 sets from both source websites
  • tldrlegal_v1.json - 84 sets from TL;DRLegal
  • tosdr_annotated_v1.json - 84 annotated sets from ToS;DR

Citation

If you use this dataset, please cite:

Manor, Laura and Li, Junyi Jessy. "Plain English Summarization of Contracts." Proceedings of the Natural Legal Language Processing Workshop 2019, Association for Computational Linguistics, 2019.
